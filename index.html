Legal & Attribution

Non-commercial fan preservation

This archive is a non-commercial, educational fan project dedicated to preserving the history of Operation Flashpoint: Cold War Crisis. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Bohemia Interactive in any way. Operation Flashpoint and its related marks are property of their respective owners.

The media on this site — screenshots, promotional artwork, trailers, and press badges — was recovered from public internet archives (including the Wayback Machine / Internet Archive) and from long-dead community fan sites, and is displayed for archival and reference purposes. The original creators and rights holders retain full ownership.

No game files or binaries are hosted. This is a catalog and media archive only. All catalogue listings are metadata; download links are disabled.

If you are a rights holder — or the author of any fan site, mod, screenshot, or artwork shown here — and you'd like something removed, credited, or linked to its original source, contact us and it will be taken down promptly.
